FAQ
Common questions.
Does it work offline?

Almost entirely. All receipts, drives, exports, and categories are stored locally on your device and work without any internet connection.

The only part that requires connectivity is AI scanning — the receipt photo is sent to our extraction server, processed, and the result returned to your phone. If you're offline, you can still add receipts manually with full detail.

Which countries does TallyShot support?

The app supports UK, US, EU, Australia, New Zealand, and Canada out of the box. Each region sets the default currency and tax handling — UK and EU receipts include VAT in the total; US and Canada add tax on top.

You can also override the tax mode and currency manually if your situation is different.

What happens to my receipt photos?

Photos stay on your device. When you scan a receipt, the image is sent to our AI extraction proxy over HTTPS, the text data is extracted, and the photo is discarded immediately — it is never logged, stored, or linked to you in any way.

We have no server-side receipt database. There is nothing to breach.

Can I export for my accountant or for self-assessment?

Yes. The export screen lets you generate a CSV or PDF filtered by date range, category, or tax deductibility. There are templates for HMRC self-assessment summaries, reimbursement reports, and full itemised detail.

You can share the file directly from the app to email, Google Drive, WhatsApp, or anything else on your phone.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

The Free plan gives you 10 AI scans per month — enough to cover occasional business meals or ad-hoc purchases. Everything else (manual entry, export, categories, mileage) is unlimited and free forever.

Pro removes the scan limit entirely. If you scan every receipt — petrol, supermarket, coffee, invoices — Pro is the right tier. It also unlocks GPS live drive tracking and expense reports for reimbursement.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the app → Settings → Cancel subscription. This takes you directly to your Google Play subscriptions page where you can cancel in one tap. No retention pop-ups, no questionnaire, no waiting period. The subscription ends at the end of the current billing cycle.
