Everything you need to know before your first scan.

Yes — download and get 10 free AI scans with no account and no card required. Each scan includes full AI extraction of merchant, amount, date, currency, and category. Upgrade to Pro when you need more.
Paper receipts (even crumpled or faded), screen receipts, handwritten receipts, till rolls — TallyShot handles them all. If the AI isn't confident about a field, you see a focused review screen to confirm or correct just that field, rather than a generic failure.
On your device. TallyShot uses local SQLite storage — your receipts never leave your phone without your explicit action. The only data sent externally is the receipt image, sent to the AI during processing, and never stored on our servers after the response is returned.
Pro subscribers can export all receipts — or a filtered subset — as a CSV file directly from the app. The CSV includes merchant, date, amount, currency, category, and a tax-deductible flag. Share it directly to your accountant, upload it to your accounting software, or open it in Excel.
Quick Scan is a Pro feature that skips the confirmation step when AI confidence is high. For most clear receipts, TallyShot saves automatically — you just scan and walk away. For anything ambiguous, it still shows a review screen.
